People Who Give Cars

Changing Lives, One Car Donation at a Time

PWGC (People Who Give Cars) takes unused vehicles, refurbishes them, and transfers ownership completely free of charge to low-income families, single parents, and individuals striving for economic independence.

Our Organization's Mission

Reliable transportation isn't a luxury—it's a gateway to opportunity.

For thousands of families, missing a vehicle means losing access to better employment, reliable healthcare, and education. PWGC bridges this infrastructure gap by collecting donated vehicles from community members, checking them for road safety, and gifting them directly to qualified applicants who need a second chance.

The Donation Lifecycle

A simple process that turns your old vehicle into someone else's new path forward.

📝

1. Easy Submission

Donors fill out our digital intake form with vehicle details. Our organization reviews the vehicle's title and condition.

🔧

2. Multi-point Inspection

Our volunteer mechanics service the vehicle, replacing worn parts to guarantee safety and road-readiness.

🔑

3. Handing Over Keys

We review applications alongside local social service partners and transfer the clean title directly to a family in need.

Vehicle Qualifications

To ensure recipient safety and maximize project impact, our simulated nonprofit enforces specific guidelines:

  • Must have a clear title in the donor's name
  • Vehicle should be safe or capable of minor repairs
  • Must pass structural safety requirements
  • 100% tax deductible simulation receipt provided
